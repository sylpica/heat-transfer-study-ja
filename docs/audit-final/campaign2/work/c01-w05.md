### C1 — Eq. (1.1): time-derivative dots added over Q and Wk that are not in print

- Printed form (verified at 400 dpi): `Q = Wk + dU/dt` (1.1), with underbrace labels "positive toward the system" under Q, "positive away from the system" under Wk, and "positive when the system's energy increases" under dU/dt. **Neither Q nor Wk carries a dot**; the text below likewise reads "where Q is the heat transfer rate and Wk is the work transfer rate". (The k of Wk is subscript, as in the app.)
- App location: `data/sections/1-2-ja.json`, block 3 (formula, number "1.1"): expression `Q̇ = Ẇₖ + dU/dt`; the same dotted forms are used in that block's `alt`/`explanation`, and again in block 4: 「ここで Q̇ は熱移動率、Ẇₖ は仕事率です」.

The underbrace sign labels themselves are conveyed correctly in the app's alt/explanation, so the candidate is the dot glyphs only. Adjudication note: the printed undotted Q/Wk already denote rates in context, so meaning is preserved; this is flagged as a notation/glyph mismatch against print, not a meaning error.

### C2 — Refrigerator bullet: "several lesser ones" dropped
- PDF page: 20 (book p. 6), third bullet from the top (§1.1 list continued).
- Printed form: "The ice cubes for our tea are made in an electrical refrigerator. **It involves three major heat exchange processes and several lesser ones.** The major ones are the condensation of refrigerant at room temperature to reject heat, the absorption of heat from within the refrigerator by evaporating the refrigerant, and the balancing heat leakage from the room to the inside."
- App location: `data/sections/1-1-ja.json`, block 12 (list), item index 6 (7th item): 「アイスティー用の氷は電気冷蔵庫で作られる。冷媒が室温で凝縮して熱を外へ捨てること、冷媒が蒸発して冷蔵庫内から熱を吸収すること、室内から内部へ熱が漏れ込むことが、主な熱交換過程である。」
- Current form vs printed form: the three major processes are all listed (so "three major" is implicit), but the printed statement that there are also **several lesser processes** has no counterpart in the app — that content is dropped. All other elements of the bullet (condensation at room temperature rejecting heat; evaporation absorbing heat from inside; balancing heat leakage room → inside) match.
