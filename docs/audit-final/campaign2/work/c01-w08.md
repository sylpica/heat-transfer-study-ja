  - block 52: 「熱損失 Q̇_net を T で表すために…」

- Assessment: systematic glyph difference across the whole one-dimensional-conduction derivation on these two pages. The app's dotted form is the book's house style elsewhere, but on PDF 31–32 the printed symbol is undotted Q_net / Q.

### C2 — Thermal-diffusivity definition formula abridged: printed unit-cancellation chain dropped (PDF 32, book p.18)
- App location: `data/sections/1-3-ja.json` block 61 (formula, α definition).
- Current (app) form: `α ≡ k/(ρc)　（単位： m²/s、または ft²/hr）`
- Printed form: `α ≡ k/(ρc) · J/(m·s·K) · m³/kg · kg·K/J = α m²/s (or ft²/hr)` — i.e. the printed equation carries the three explicit unit factors J/(m·s·K), m³/kg, kg·K/J whose cancellation yields m²/s.
- Assessment: the app expression omits the printed dimensional chain entirely and replaces it with a units parenthesis. (The block's `explanation` field gives a dimensionally equivalent unit discussion, but the formula expression itself does not reproduce the printed equation.)
