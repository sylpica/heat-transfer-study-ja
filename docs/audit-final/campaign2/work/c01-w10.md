## Candidate discrepancies

### C1 — Fig. 1.10 alt: evaluation-bar formula rewritten with absolute-value bars (PDF 37, book p.23)
- App location: `data/sections/1-3-ja.json` block 105 (figure 1.10), `alt` field.
- Current (app) form: 「物体内部の温度変化は、表面における L|dT/dx| = (h̄L/k_b)(T_b − T_∞) より小さい」
- Printed form (caption, verified at 400 dpi): "The temperature variation within the body is less than L dT/dx |_surface = (h̄L/k_b)(T_b − T_∞)" — i.e. printed has **no leading bar** before dT/dx; the only bar is the single trailing evaluation bar with subscript "surface".
- Assessment: the app converts the printed evaluation notation `L dT/dx|_surface` into an absolute value `L|dT/dx|` and drops the evaluation-bar/subscript form. (The absolute value arguably makes the printed magnitude statement explicit — the surface gradient itself is negative in the figure, ∂T/∂x|_surface = −q/k_body = −(T_b − T_∞)h̄/k_b — but the notation differs from print.)
