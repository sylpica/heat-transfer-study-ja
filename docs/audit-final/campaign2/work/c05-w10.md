### C1 — PDF 309 (book 295) — prose — integral numbering in the (6.36) explanation is self-contradictory
- App location: `data/sections/6-3-ja.json` block [8], `explanation` of formula (6.36).
- Current form (short verbatim): "第1の積分は内部エネルギーと流動仕事が R から出る率、第2の積分は R からの正味熱伝導率、第3項は R 内の発熱率です。第2の積分において、(u⃗·n⃗)dS は検査面の要素 dS を通る体積流量を表します。"
- Printed form (short verbatim): underbraces label the (ρĥ) integral "rate of internal energy and flow work out of R" and the (−k∇T) integral "net heat conduction rate out of R"; prose: "In the second integral, u⃗ · n⃗ dS represents the volume flow rate…" — i.e. printed counts all integrals in order, so the (ρĥ) integral carrying u⃗·n⃗ is the **second** integral.
- Problem: the app explanation first counts only the RHS integrals (enthalpy integral = 第1, conduction integral = 第2), then in its last sentence calls the enthalpy integral 第2 following the printed counting. The same integral is labelled both 第1 and 第2 within one explanation, and under the explanation's own first counting the "(u⃗·n⃗)dS" sentence points at the conduction integral, which does not contain u⃗·n⃗.

### C2 — PDF 311 (book 297) — formula — evaluation subscript η=0 scope in the h derivation
- App location: `data/sections/6-3-ja.json` block [19], paragraph (inline h formula).
- Current form (short verbatim): "h = [k/(T∞ − Tw)]·∂(T − Tw)/∂y|y=0 = k·(∂f′/∂η)(∂η/∂y)|η=0"
- Printed form (short verbatim): "h = k/(T∞ − Tw) · ∂(T − Tw)/∂y |_{y=0} = k ( ∂f′/∂η · ∂η/∂y )_{η=0}"
- Problem (minor / notation): in print the subscript η=0 sits outside the closing parenthesis and evaluates the whole product (∂f′/∂η)(∂η/∂y); in the app's linear form "|η=0" follows only the last factor (∂η/∂y), so the factor that actually needs η=0, ∂f′/∂η (= d²f/dη²), is not marked as evaluated. Meaning is recoverable, but the evaluation scope differs from print.
