## Candidate discrepancies (5)

### C1 — Fig. 6.15 caption: approximation glyph ≊ vs printed ≅
- PDF p. 321 (book p. 307), Figure 6.15 caption.
- App: `6-5-ja.json` block 22 (figure 6.15, caption): "熱境界層のほぼ全体で u **≊** u∞ である"
- Printed: "so thin that u **≅** u∞ in almost the entire thermal b.l." — 400-dpi crop confirms tilde OVER the equals sign (≅, U+2245), not ≊ (U+224A).
- Kind: glyph substitution (approximation symbol).

### C2 — Pr condition glyph in eqs. (6.64) and (6.68): app ≧ vs printed approx-greater-or-equal glyph
- PDF p. 322 (6.64) and PDF p. 323 (6.68).
- App: block 29 (eq. 6.64) "Pr **≧** 0.6"; block 36 (eq. 6.68) "Pr **≧** 0.6" (≧ = U+2267).
- Printed: both conditions show a single curved ≻-over-equals glyph (⪰-type, "approximately ≥"), confirmed at 400 dpi in both places. It is visibly distinct from the prose glyph on PDF p. 321, "Pe_x ≳ 100", where a straight ≥ carries a separate tilde above it (app block 25 explanation renders that one correctly as ≳).
- Kind: glyph substitution (relation symbol). Note: same app ≧ convention appears in out-of-packet eq. (6.58) (block 17); flagged here only for the two in-packet instances.
