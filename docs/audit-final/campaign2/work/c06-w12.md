### C06-W12-1 — Eqs. (7.43a) and (7.43b): book prints Re with subscript *d* (lowercase), app writes Re_D

- PDF page: 385 (book p. 371), eqs. (7.43a) and (7.43b). Confirmed at 400 dpi:
  in both equations the Reynolds-number subscript is a lowercase italic *d*
  sitting below the baseline — visibly different from the capital *D* used in
  Nu_D on the same lines and in Re_D in the sentence immediately below
  ("for 2300 ⩽ Re_D ⩽ 5 × 10⁶").
- App location: `data/sections/7-3-ja.json` block 29 (eq. 7.43a) and block 30 (eq. 7.43b).
- Current form (app, exact):
  - Block 29: `NuD = 0.0214(ReD^0.8 − 100)Pr^0.4、0.6 ≦ Pr ≦ 1.5`
  - Block 30: `NuD = 0.012(ReD^0.87 − 280)Pr^0.4、1.5 ≦ Pr ≦ 500`
- Printed form (book, exact):
  - (7.43a): `Nu_D = 0.0214(Re_d^0.8 − 100)Pr^0.4,  0.6 ⩽ Pr ⩽ 1.5`
  - (7.43b): `Nu_D = 0.012(Re_d^0.87 − 280)Pr^0.4,  1.5 ⩽ Pr ⩽ 500`
- Everything else in both equations (coefficients 0.0214 / 0.012, exponents
  0.8 / 0.87 on Re only, −100 / −280 inside the parentheses, Pr^0.4, Pr ranges)
  matches exactly.
- Suggested severity: **P3** (subscript glyph). The book's Re_d is almost
  certainly its own notational slip (d is undefined here; the app's Re_D is the
  symbol used everywhere else in the book), so the app form may be a deliberate
  normalisation — flagged for coordinator adjudication rather than asserted as
  an error.

### C06-W12-2 — Eq. (7.50) Haaland equation: app inserts a minus sign before 1.8 that the book does not print

- PDF page: 387 (book p. 373), eq. (7.50). Confirmed at 400 dpi: the book
  prints `f = 1 / {1.8 log₁₀[ 6.9/Re_D + ((ε/D)/3.7)^1.11 ]}²` — positive 1.8,
  no minus sign anywhere, written as a reciprocal over a squared brace.
- App location: `data/sections/7-3-ja.json` block 40 (formula, number "7.50").
- Current form (app, exact):
  `f = {−1.8·log10[6.9/ReD + ((ε/D)/3.7)^1.11]}^(−2)`
- Printed form (book, exact):
  `f = 1/{1.8 log₁₀[6.9/Re_D + ((ε/D)/3.7)^1.11]}²`
- Note for adjudication: the two forms are numerically identical for all
  inputs, because (−1.8·log₁₀[…])^(−2) = 1/(1.8·log₁₀[…])² (even power). The
  app's −1.8 form is the Haaland equation as usually quoted in the literature,
  but it is **not** the form printed in this book. The bracket structure
  ((ε/D)/3.7)^1.11, 6.9/Re_D and the validity ranges in the block's explanation
  (0 ⩽ ε/D ⩽ 0.05, 4000 ⩽ Re_D ⩽ 10⁸) all match the book.
- Suggested severity: **P2** (displayed-formula deviation from source; value-neutral).
