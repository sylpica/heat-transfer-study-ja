### C07-W01-1 — PDF p.409 (book p.395), eq. (7.73h) LHS missing mean overline in §7.6 explanation
- App location: data/sections/7-6-ja.json, block 25 (formula 7.72), field `explanation`, the (7.73h) clause.
- Current form (exact quote): "NuD = 0.027(ST/SL)^0.2·ReD^0.8（Pr = 0.7、式(7.73h)）" — the "Nu" here is codepoints U+004E U+0075 with no combining macron (verified by codepoint dump; the same block's `expression` for eq. (7.72) and the §7.6 lesson both write it correctly with U+0304).
- Printed form (exact quote): "Nū_D = 0.027(S_T/S_L)^0.2 Re_D^0.8, Pr = 0.7 (7.73h)" — the overline spans "Nu" (Nū), i.e. a mean Nusselt number, and in this equation the LHS changes from fn(Re_D) (7.73a–g) to Nū_D.
- What differs: the app's (7.73h) clause in block 25's explanation drops the combining macron U+0304 over "Nu", writing plain "NuD" where the book (and the app itself elsewhere) has the mean symbol Nū_D. All coefficients, the (S_T/S_L)^0.2 bracket power, Re_D^0.8, and the Pr = 0.7 condition match.
