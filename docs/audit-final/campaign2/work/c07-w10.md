### C7 — PDF p.448 — exponent 1/3 dropped from Ra_L in the proportionality statement (statement as written is false)
- App location: `data/sections/8-4-ja.json`, block 16 (paragraph, inclined and horizontal plates)
- Current form: `後者の場合、NuL と RaL はどちらも L に比例するため、熱伝達係数は L に対して独立である。`
- Printed form: `In the latter case, both Nu_L and Ra_L^{1/3} are proportional to L, so the heat transfer coefficient is independent of L.` (book p. 434)
- What differs: the `^(1/3)` on Ra_L is dropped. Ra_L itself is proportional to L³, not to L; only Ra_L^{1/3} is proportional to L. The app's stated reason for h being independent of L is therefore wrong as written, although the conclusion (h independent of L) matches print.

### C8 — PDF p.448 — deep lesson attributes plume formation to the stable side; print attributes it to the unstable faces
- App location: `data/original-deep-lessons.json`, lessons[46] (§8.4), block 17
- Current form: `水平に近づく極限では安定側でもプルームが立つため、単純な置き換えの範囲を超える。`
- Printed form: `In the limit θ = 90° — a horizontal plate — the fluid flow above a hot plate or below a cold plate must form one or more plumes, as shown in Figs. 8.8c and d. In such cases, the b.l. is unstable for all but small Rayleigh numbers…` (book p. 434)
- What differs: the book assigns plume formation at θ = 90° to the flow above a hot plate / below a cold plate — the unstable faces in this passage (the stable faces are the lower surface of a heated plate and the upper surface of a cooled plate). The lesson's added sentence claims plumes also arise on the stable side, which the passage does not state and which contradicts its stable/unstable classification.
