### C2 — §8.5 deep lesson overstates the noncondensable-gas penalty as “half or less”
- PDF page: 469 (book p. 455)
- App location: `data/original-deep-lessons.json`, lessons[47] (sectionId “8.5”), block 13, heading + text
- Current form (exact quote): heading `非凝縮ガスの定量目安：質量1%の空気で h が半分以下`; text `水蒸気中に質量でわずか1%の空気が混ざっているだけで、熱伝達係数 h は半分以下に低下しうる。`
- Printed form (exact quote): `The resulting h can easily be cut by a factor of two if there is as little as 1% by mass of air mixed into the steam [8.41].`
- What differs: Printed says h is cut *by a factor of two* (i.e. reduced to one half). The lesson says h drops to “半分以下” (one half *or less*), stating a stronger penalty than the book gives. The §8.5 section translation (8-5-ja.json block 36) renders the same sentence correctly as “生じる h は容易に半分に減ることがある”, so the lesson deviates from both the book and the section text.
