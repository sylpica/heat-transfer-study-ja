## (a) Candidate discrepancies

### C1 — "node" rendered as 腹 (antinode) — terminology inversion
- PDF p.500 (book p.486), §9.3 prose: "The heavy phase falls down at one **node** of a wave
  and the light fluid rises into the other **node**."
- App: `data/sections/9-3-ja.json` block 2: 「重い相は波の1つの**腹（node）**で下方に落ち、
  軽い流体はもう1つの**腹**へ上昇する」
- Printed vs app: wave "node" in standard Japanese terminology is 節, whereas 腹 denotes the
  antinode/loop — the opposite point of a standing wave. The app even glosses 腹 with "(node)",
  so the Japanese term and its own gloss contradict each other. Candidate: 腹 → 節 (both instances).

### C2 — Figure 9.7 internal content (axes + legend) not represented anywhere in the app
- PDF p.499 (book p.485), Figure 9.7 (placed at the head of §9.3's first page, content belongs to §9.2).
  Judged at 400 dpi from the rendered image:
  - y-axis: (q/(μ·h_fg))·√(σ/(g(ρ_f−ρ_g))), log scale 0.1–100 (radical covers σ/(g(ρ_f−ρ_g)) only).
  - x-axis: c_p(T_w−T_sat)/(h_fg·Pr^1.0), log scale 0.005–0.3.
  - Data series legend: ○ 1 atm, △ 2.6 atm, ● 52.4 atm, ▲ 82 atm, × 109 atm, □ 167.7 atm;
    straight line labelled "eqn. (9.4)".
- App: `data/sections/9-2-ja.json` block 17 (figure 9.7): caption is a correct translation
  ("直径0.61 mm の白金線上で沸騰する水のデータに適用された、ローゼノウの相関式[9.13]の図解"),
  but alt ("ローゼノウ式とデータの比較図") and description ("散らばりが大きいことが示される") carry
  none of the axis groups or the six pressure series, and neither lesson lessons[49] nor any
  9.2/9.3 question supplies them. Low-severity completeness candidate (alt/description do not
  misstate the figure; the internal data are simply absent).

### C3 — Footnote 3: "most susceptible to collapse" softened to 「最も影響を受けやすい」
- PDF p.500 footnote 3: "the wavelength most **susceptible to collapse** is the most
  'dangerous' one" (i.e. the wavelength most prone to collapse).
- App: `data/sections/9-3-ja.json` block 3 (footnote): 「崩壊に最も**影響を受けやすい**波長が
  最も「危険な（dangerous）」ものである」
- 「影響を受けやすい」 ("easily influenced") shifts the predicate from collapsibility
  （崩壊しやすい) to susceptibility to influence. Minor semantic candidate; the "dangerous"
  point itself is preserved.

### C4 — §9.3 heading: "Peak" rendered as 限界 (low confidence, likely intentional)
- PDF p.499 heading: "9.3 **Peak** pool boiling heat flux".
- App: `data/sections/9-3-ja.json` block 0: 「9.3 プール沸騰の**限界**熱流束」.
- The body (block 2) correctly uses ピーク熱流束 throughout, and 限界熱流束 is the standard
  Japanese CHF term, so this is probably a deliberate heading choice; flagged only so the
  coordinator can confirm it is settled style rather than drift.
