### C1 — Prose before eq. (11.129): the substitution "x_i v⃗_i = N⃗_i/c" is transposed in the app (PDF 717, book p.703)
- App location: `data/sections/11-11-ja.json` block 8 (paragraph), final portion for this page.
- Current (app) form: 「Nv⃗i = xi·v⃗i/c、xi = pi/p と置き」 — literally "N·v⃗_i = x_i·v⃗_i/c" (N carries the combining arrow U+20D7 together with v: "Nv⃗i").
- Printed form (verified at 200 dpi and in the intro-line crop): "Upon putting x_i v⃗_i = N⃗_i/c, x_i = p_i/p, and setting the total pressure p constant…" — i.e. LHS is x_i·v⃗_i and RHS is N⃗_i/c.
- Assessment: as written, the app sentence equates N v⃗_i with x_i v⃗_i / c, which is not the printed relation (and not dimensionally the definition N⃗_i = c x_i v⃗_i that the printed form states). The equation's two sides appear transposed/garbled around the N. 要確認.

The app normalises (11.132) to the (11.131) form. Flagged as a book inconsistency for adjudication, not assumed to be an app error.
