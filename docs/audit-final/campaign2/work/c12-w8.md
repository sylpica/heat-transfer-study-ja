- ᵃ Btu footnote: British thermal unit originally defined as the heat that raises 1 lbm of
  water 1°F; several values depending on initial temperature; the tabulated value is the
  International Table (i.e., steam table) Btu; a "mean" Btu of 1055.87 J is also common;

  calorie of 4.184 J has also been in common use; the dietitian's "Calorie" is actually 1 kilocalorie.
